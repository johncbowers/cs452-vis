Dijkstra’s algorithm as a wavefront

The next closest vertex crosses from the unprocessed side to the processed side. Relaxing its tense outgoing edges advances the wavefront.

Tense edge u → v is tense when dist(u) + w(u → v) < dist(v).

    adapted from Erickson, Chapter 8
    1. InitSSSP(s)
    2. Insert(s, 0)
    3. while the priority queue is not empty
    4. u ← ExtractMin()
    5. for each edge u → v
    6. if u → v is tense
    7. Relax(u → v)
    8. Insert(v) or DecreaseKey(v)

    What to watch: With nonnegative edge weights, extracted distances never decrease. Once a vertex crosses the wavefront, its distance is final.
